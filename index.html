WHAT DRIVES MY WORK

.research interests

THREE CONNECTED DIRECTIONS

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, clinical needs, and real-world deployment—addressing both how medical AI is developed and how it can reach people.

01 /

Accessible medical imaging AI

Lightweight models and on-device inference for clinical image screening, with practical considerations for offline use and resource-limited settings.

02 /

Federated & privacy-preserving AI

Collaborative learning across institutions and devices, supporting model development while keeping sensitive image datasets at their original sites.

03 /

Trustworthy & data-centric AI

Robustness across populations and sites, meaningful evaluation, uncertainty, and responsible pathways from research models to clinical practice.
