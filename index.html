HELLO, I'M

Lena Duraisamy
Swamikannan, Ph.D.

Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering · Healthcare AI Researcher

I build privacy-preserving and deployment-ready AI for healthcare, with a focus on medical imaging, federated learning, and smartphone-based screening technologies.

I earned my Ph.D. at The University of Texas at Dallas, where my research explored how institutions can collaboratively develop clinically meaningful AI without transferring patient images between sites. My goal is to bridge the gap between AI research and clinical translation, developing trustworthy, scalable AI models that can be effectively deployed in real-world healthcare settings to make high-quality care more accessible, affordable, and equitable, regardless of geographical location.

WHAT DRIVES MY WORK

.research interests

THREE CONNECTED DIRECTIONS

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, clinical needs, and real-world deployment—addressing both how medical AI is developed and how it can reach people.

01 /

Accessible medical imaging AI

Lightweight models and on-device inference for clinical image screening, with practical considerations for offline use and resource-limited settings.

02 /

Federated & privacy-preserving AI

Collaborative learning across institutions and devices, supporting model development while keeping sensitive image datasets at their original sites.

03 /

Trustworthy & data-centric AI

Robustness across populations and sites, meaningful evaluation, uncertainty, and responsible pathways from research models to clinical practice.
